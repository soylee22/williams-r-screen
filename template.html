Before you buy · read this

i.Oversold gets more oversold. A −80 print above a rising 200-day mean is often the first leg of a breakdown; the average lags a top by months. The trend filter reduces this risk, it does not remove it.

ii.The dip may be information. Fresh, deep prints cluster on earnings gaps and bad news. Establish why it fell before treating the fall as noise; earnings dates are not in this table.

iii.Correlated firing. In a correction hundreds of rows light at once. That is one market bet, not many independent ones.

iv.Candidates, not edge. Nothing here is backtested as a signal. Sizing, the regime gate and the discipline to hold decide returns.

Method · in brief

%R = (HH − C) ÷ (HH − LL) × −100 over fourteen bars; the close that prints level L is T = (1−p)·HH + p·LL, p = |L|/100.

The weekly reading is evaluated on every daily close (the forming week plus thirteen complete ones), so freshness is counted in trading days. An episode tolerates a single day's pop above −80. Chronic means in the zone more than a quarter of the past two years. Triggers are exact for an orderly close onto the level; a capitulation that widens the range puts the true trigger lower.
